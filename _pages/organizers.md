### Organizers

**Hung Le** (Main Coordinator) — Senior Lecturer and ARC DECRA Fellow at Deakin Applied Artificial Intelligence Initiative (A2I2), Deakin University, Australia. At A2I2, he has supervised PhD students and led research on foundation models, agentic AI, reinforcement learning, memory-augmented learning, and multimodal AI, with publications in leading venues including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, ACL, and KDD. He has served as Area Chair, Senior Program Committee member, Meta-Reviewer, and Reviewer for major conferences including AAAI, ICML, ICLR, ACL, and NeurIPS, and has organized tutorials at KDD, IJCAI, AJCAI, and AAMAS. His expertise is closely related to this workshop, particularly in efficient foundation models, memory-augmented agents, reasoning and planning, and resource-efficient AI systems. He is committed to supporting an inclusive research community through mentoring students, promoting international collaboration, and supporting early-career researchers. [Homepage](https://thaihungle.github.io/)

**Haripriya Harikumar** (Submission and Review Coordinator) — Research Fellow at the Centre for AI Fundamentals at University of Manchester, UK. She received her Ph.D. degree in Machine Learning from Pattern Recognition and Data Analytics at Deakin University, Australia. Her research focuses on developing secure, safe, and trustworthy AI systems that can make robust decisions and reliable predictions, even in uncertain and resource-constrained environments. She aims to enhance the reliability of AI-driven technologies, including healthcare tools, online platforms, and autonomous systems, making them safer and more dependable for widespread use. Her papers were accepted at reputed conferences in Machine Learning, Data Mining and Computer Vision. She has served as a reviewer in top conferences and workshops, including NeurIPS, ICML, UAI, CVPR, ECCV, BMVC, ICCV, and AAAI. She also co-organized and successfully executed the accepted NeurIPS 2023 workshop on [Backdoors in Deep Learning](https://neurips2023-bugs.github.io). [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=50ErN80AAAAJ) · [Email](mailto:haripriya.harikumar@manchester.ac.uk)

**Khanh-Binh (Calvin) Nguyen** (Coordinator for Invited Talks) — Associate Research Fellow at Deakin Applied Artificial Intelligence Initiative (A2I2) at Deakin University, Australia. He earned his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Sungkyunkwan University. His core research investigates advanced computer vision, Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and the engineering of intelligent AI agents. Through his work on memory-friendly designs, Multi-Agent Debate (MAD) systems, and latent reasoning pipelines, he actively aims to advance the accessibility of AI beyond traditional frontier models. His research is published in top-tier venues, and he regularly serves as a reviewer and Program Committee member for premier conferences and journals, including NeurIPS, CVPR, AAAI, DAC, ICCAD, DATE, and IEEE Transactions on Multimedia. He is committed to supporting an inclusive research community through mentoring students and promoting international collaboration. ### Previous Experience Organizing a Workshop

The organizing team has extensive experience organizing workshops, tutorials, seminars, and community-building activities in machine learning and artificial intelligence. Collectively, the organizers have coordinated events at leading international conferences spanning machine learning, artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning, foundation models, multimodal learning, and trustworthy AI.

Relevant experience includes:

* **NeurIPS 2023 Workshop:** [Backdoors in Deep Learning: The Good, the Bad and the Ugly](https://neurips2023-bugs.github.io)
  * Relevant organizer: Haripriya Harikumar
  * The workshop brought together researchers working on AI security, robustness, and trustworthy machine learning, and attracted participants from both academia and industry.

* **AAMAS 2024 Tutorial:** Unlocking Exploration: Self-Motivated Agents Thrive on Memory-Driven Curiosity
  * Relevant organizer: Hung Le
  * The tutorial focused on memory-augmented agents, intrinsic motivation, and reinforcement learning.

* **AJCAI 2024 Tutorial:** Towards Safe and Controlled Large Language Models
  * Relevant organizers: Hung Le and Manh Nguyen
  * The tutorial covered alignment, safety, controllability, and evaluation of large language models.

* **AJCAI 2022 Tutorial:** Memory-Based Reinforcement Learning
  * Relevant organizer: Hung Le
  * The tutorial introduced memory architectures and long-horizon decision-making in reinforcement learning.

* **IJCAI 2021 Tutorial:** Neural Machine Reasoning
  * Relevant organizer: Hung Le
  * The tutorial focused on reasoning, memory, and knowledge integration in neural systems.

* **KDD 2021 Tutorial:** From Deep Learning to Deep Reasoning
  * Relevant organizer: Hung Le
  * The tutorial explored emerging approaches for integrating reasoning capabilities into deep learning systems.

Beyond event organization, members of the organizing team have served as Area Chairs, Senior Program Committee members, Meta-Reviewers (Hung Le), and Reviewers (Haripriya Harikumar, Calvin Nguyen, Dung Nguyen, Long Dang) for premier venues including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, ACL, EMNLP, KDD, IJCAI, and AAMAS. These experiences provide strong expertise in scientific program management, peer review coordination, community building, and research leadership.

The organizing team brings complementary expertise in foundation models, agentic AI, reasoning and planning, memory-augmented systems, multimodal learning, trustworthy AI, and efficient machine learning. These research areas closely align with the workshop's focus on developing open and efficient agentic AI systems that can operate effectively under computational, deployment, and resource constraints.

The team will ensure effective organization through a clear division of responsibilities, including invited speaker coordination, paper review management, publicity and community outreach, website development and maintenance, program scheduling, and communication with the ACML workshop chairs. Drawing on prior experience organizing workshops, tutorials, and conference activities, the team is well positioned to deliver a high-quality forum that promotes discussion, networking, and collaboration among researchers working on open and efficient agentic AI.
