### Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Plan

The OE-Agent workshop is committed to fostering an inclusive and welcoming environment for researchers, practitioners, and students from diverse backgrounds, institutions, career stages, and geographic regions. Consistent with the workshop theme of open and efficient agentic AI for resource-constrained environments, we aim to promote research that enables broader participation in AI beyond organizations with access to frontier-scale computational resources.

We will actively seek diversity among invited speakers, panelists, authors, reviewers, and participants. In particular, we will strive for balanced representation across academia, industry, and government research organizations, as well as participation from different countries and regions within the Asia-Pacific community. We will also make efforts to include speakers and contributors from both established research centers and emerging AI ecosystems, reflecting the workshop's emphasis on accessible and deployable AI.

The workshop will encourage participation from early-career researchers, PhD students, and under-represented communities by providing opportunities for oral presentations, poster sessions, networking, and interaction with invited speakers. We will also seek diversity in research perspectives, including foundation models, agent architectures, planning and reasoning, reinforcement learning, retrieval and memory systems, multimodal AI, AI systems, and real-world deployment.

By bringing together participants with diverse experiences and viewpoints, the workshop aims to foster meaningful discussion, encourage new collaborations, and strengthen the Asia-Pacific research community working on open and efficient agentic AI.
