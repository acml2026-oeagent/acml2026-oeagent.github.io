### Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Consideration

A major goal of the workshop is to foster broad and inclusive participation across its organizers, invited speakers, Program Committee, and attendees. We aim to engage researchers and practitioners from diverse research and educational communities, career stages, and professional sectors, including students, academics, industry researchers, and practitioners. We also seek broad representation across geographic regions, genders, ethnicities, and backgrounds.

The Organizing Committee reflects this commitment to diversity, with members from institutions across Asia, Australia, and Europe, representing a range of career stages from early-career researchers to senior academics. Our invited speakers are drawn from diverse genders, ethnicities, and institutional affiliations spanning both academia and industry. Likewise, the Program Committee comprises researchers and practitioners from academia and industry with diverse expertise, professional roles, genders, and backgrounds.
